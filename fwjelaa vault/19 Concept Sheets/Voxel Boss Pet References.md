Fifteen in-game screenshots of five blocky, voxel-style pets and bosses, added in November 2026. Open them with the Read tool before modelling a pet or boss in this style. They show the target look, and the notes under each one say what reads well and what doesn't. For the method, see [[Organic and Creature Modeling]], [[Shape Language and Silhouette Testing]] and [[Critiquing a Model - Paintovers and Iteration]].

## The style
- **Bodies:** stacked cubes and slabs with a visible voxel step, and no smooth curves.
- **Colour:** one base value per model (near-black charcoal, royal blue, white and lavender) plus one glowing accent hue (orange fire, cyan lightning, neon green).
- **Where the accent goes:** where the eye should land: eyes, mouths, horn tips, a chest emblem, crack lines, ankle bands.
- **Flames:** separate flat flame shapes on horns, backs and tail tips.
- **Scale:** the Flaming Bull's sign reads "Egg 2, 8.7 studs". The other signs can't be read at this size, but the Cerberus and the Yeti Mother stand well above a player.

## Inferno Cerberus
![[Pet Ref - Inferno Cerberus - front three-quarter.png]]
![[Pet Ref - Inferno Cerberus - front.png]]
![[Pet Ref - Inferno Cerberus - side.png]]
- **What it is:** a three-headed charcoal dog with glowing orange mouths and yellow eyes, a spiked lavender collar, a yellow sun emblem on the chest, orange crack lines on the legs, flame tufts along the back and a flame tail tip.
- **Reads well:** the chest emblem, the back flames and the glowing mouths.
- **Weak:** from the front the three dark heads merge into one mass, and the hairline crack lines disappear at a distance.

## Flaming Bull
![[Pet Ref - Flaming Bull - front.png]]
![[Pet Ref - Flaming Bull - three-quarter.png]]
![[Pet Ref - Flaming Bull - side.png]]
- **What it is:** a charcoal bull with bone-white horns that end in flames, orange crack lines on the flanks and orange bands above the hooves.
- **Reads well:** the white horns with flame tips are the strongest shape in the set.
- **Weak:** the lowered, dusty-mauve head reads as a separate box. The eyes are tiny, so the bull has no face from the front. The body is a plain box on four posts.

## Lightning Dragon
![[Pet Ref - Lightning Dragon - three-quarter.png]]
![[Pet Ref - Lightning Dragon - side.png]]
![[Pet Ref - Lightning Dragon - front wings spread.png]]
- **What it is:** an upright royal-blue dragon (its name isn't shown) with dark navy wing bones, blue membranes veined with cyan lightning, cyan crystal spikes and white teeth.
- **Reads well:** the strongest of the five. The silhouette is clear from the front and the side, and the dark bones against the bright membranes carry the theme.
- **Weak:** the neck is a stack of separate blocks, and the eyes don't stand out.

## Green Hydra
![[Pet Ref - Green Hydra - front.png]]
![[Pet Ref - Green Hydra - side.png]]
![[Pet Ref - Green Hydra - top down.png]]
- **What it is:** a three-headed green hydra (its name isn't shown) with neon-green highlights, dark purple-grey back plates and yellow-and-black hazard stripes around the necks.
- **Reads well:** the neon-green glow, and the three heads seen from above.
- **Weak:** the hazard stripes read as construction tape, not as part of a creature. From the top, the necks and the tail look like loose blocks with gaps between them. The front view is cluttered.

## Yeti Mother
![[Pet Ref - Yeti Mother - front.png]]
![[Pet Ref - Yeti Mother - three-quarter.png]]
![[Pet Ref - Yeti Mother - rear three-quarter.png]]
- **What it is:** a white and lavender yeti with dark drip-shaped fur tips, huge ice-blue horns with brown bases, ice crystals on the shoulders, a dark blue face with angry eyes and fangs, and blue ice feet. A smaller "Blizzard Yeti (pet)" stands next to it.
- **Reads well:** the dark face is the focal point, and the dark fur tips outline the body.
- **Weak:** white on a white floor or on snow loses its edges. From behind, the body is a shapeless mass of cubes. A small orange crack on one shin is off-palette.

## Common mistakes
- Avoid: several dark heads with nothing to tell them apart -> give each head its own glowing eyes and mouth, and space the heads apart
- Avoid: hairline accent lines -> make crack lines and veins thick enough to survive at real in-game size
- Avoid: a face too small to see -> make the eyes big and bright enough to read from the front
- Avoid: neck and tail segments with gaps between them -> overlap the segments or sleeve them into each other
- Avoid: a light base colour in a light zone -> check contrast against the floor and lighting of the zone the pet lives in
- Avoid: a second accent hue that doesn't belong -> one glowing accent per model

## Checklist
- One base colour and one glowing accent hue.
- Silhouette readable from the front, the side and the high three-quarter camera.
- Face readable from the front.
- Accent lines still visible at the real in-game size.
- Tested against the zone's floor and lighting, not only on a white baseplate.
