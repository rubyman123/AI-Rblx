# debate

`/fwjelaa:debate` picks a Roblox game concept in five rounds: five candidates, independent scores from the
market, revenue, retention and scope agents, a red team against the top two, rebuttals, then a binding SHIP or
KILL verdict written to `docs/VERDICT.md`. Weights: retention 30%, market timing 25%, scope 25%, revenue 20%.

Agents: `fwjelaa:market-analyst`, `fwjelaa:revenue-modeler`, `fwjelaa:retention-architect`,
`fwjelaa:scope-auditor`, `fwjelaa:red-team`, `fwjelaa:arbiter`.
