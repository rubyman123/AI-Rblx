# make-map

Builds a finished, walkable Roblox map in the open Studio place: blocky simulator maps, backrooms, realistic
horror houses at night and nature (terrain, lakes, HDRI skies). It plans in five lines, builds phase by phase
with the MapKit kit, then checks screenshots against a rubric, walks the map and measures the triangle and
draw-call budget.

- Plugin skill: `skills/make-map/` (start with its `CLAUDE.md` and `qa.md`)
- Examples: lift-a-boulder, backrooms-level-0, farmhouse-night, forest-lake, office-floor-14
- Measured facts: `skills/make-map/research/00-measured.md`
